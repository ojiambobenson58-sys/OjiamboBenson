# Case 1: Premium-format dilemma

Cinema: CIN001

## Management brief
Evaluate whether premium revenue compensates for high energy and maintenance costs.

## Analyst assignment
Compare format-level retained revenue, usage, energy and direct contribution; explicitly allocate shared staff/rent.

## Required recommendation
Present a premium price sensitivity and equipment efficiency investment case.

## Deliverables
A one-page management memo, reconciled monthly KPI workbook, two diagnostic charts, a quantified scenario and a risk register. Cite calculation grain, distinguish association from causation, and state missing information.

## Assessment
30% financial reconciliation, 25% diagnostic evidence, 25% decision economics, 20% limitations and communication. Validate descriptive outputs against executive_results.json.
